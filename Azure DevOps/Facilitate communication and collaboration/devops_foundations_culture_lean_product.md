# 🧠 The 12 DevOps Mindsets

## 1. Leadership Mindset
Leaders must be:
- Visionary
- Adaptive
- Empowering
- Accountable
- Motivating

## 2. Customer‑Focused Mindset
- Listen to customers
- Measure what matters
- Build → measure → learn
- Embrace production issues (own the red)
- Use feature toggles & data‑driven decisions

## 3. Lean Thinking
- **Value**: define what customers value
- **Value Stream**: map product lifecycle
- **Flow**: remove delays
- **Pull**: deliver only what is needed
- **Perfection**: continuous improvement

## 4. Systems Thinking
Focus on the **whole value stream**, not silos.
IT → Dev → Ops → Customer → Feedback

## 5. Removing Waste
Seven wastes:
- Partially done work
- Extra processing
- Extra features
- Task switching
- Waiting
- Motion
- Defects

## 6. Theory of Constraints
Steps:
1. Identify constraint
2. Exploit it
3. Subordinate to it
4. Elevate it
5. Repeat

Constraints determine system throughput.

## 7. Balancing Alignment & Autonomy
Too much alignment → rigidity
Too much autonomy → chaos

Goal: aligned vision + autonomous execution

## 8. Shift‑Left Testing
Move testing earlier in lifecycle → fewer defects, faster delivery.

## 9. Security Mindset
- Promote awareness
- Define secure principles
- Enforce them

## 10. Hypothesis‑Driven Development
Build small experiments:
- Assume → Test → Measure → Learn

## 11. Live‑Site Mindset
Production is the source of truth.
Principles:
- Detect issues before users do
- Drive decisions with data
- Automate everything
- Root cause focus

## 12. Measure Outcomes, Not Activity
Measure:
- Usage
- Velocity
- Reliability
- Customer happiness

NOT:
- Lines of code
- Burndown charts
- Bug counts

# 🛍 DevOps Lean Product Management
Great product companies share:
- Speed to market
- Agility
- Customer obsession
- Strong digital experiences

## Why Shift to a Product‑Centric Model?
By 2024:
- **75%+** of digital leaders benefit from product management
- **80%** of IT orgs restructured around product teams

## What Is a Product‑Centric Model?
A product is:
- A customer‑facing capability
- Continuously evolving
- Owned by a **Product Manager**
- Funded continuously

A product is **not** a project.
- Projects have end dates and fixed scope
- Products evolve indefinitely

### Benefits
- Faster iteration
- Knowledge retention
- Higher team motivation
- Architectural integrity
- Better customer outcomes
- Shorter cycle times

## Full‑Stack Product Team
Roles:
- Product Manager
- Product Owner
- Scrum Master
- Engineers
- Site Reliability Engineer
- Agile Architect

Teams are flatter, cross‑functional, and own the product end‑to‑end.
